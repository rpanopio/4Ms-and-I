What We Offer

Comprehensive Care,
Right Where You Are

Our licensed Physical Therapists bring hospital-quality care to your living room, bedroom, or wherever you're most comfortable.

🔍

Initial Evaluation

A thorough in-home assessment of your condition, mobility, pain levels, and goals. We create a personalized treatment plan designed for your space and lifestyle.

Most Requested
💪

Hands-On Treatment

Manual therapy, therapeutic exercises, stretching, and functional movement training — all performed in your home with professional equipment we bring to you.

🏥

Post-Surgery Recovery

Structured rehabilitation after joint replacement, orthopedic surgery, or cardiac procedures. Safe, progressive recovery tailored to your surgeon's protocol.

🧓

Senior & Fall Prevention

Balance training, strength building, and fall-risk reduction for older adults. We help seniors stay independent, confident, and safe in their own homes.

🧠

Neurological Rehab

Specialized care for stroke recovery, Parkinson's disease, MS, and other neurological conditions — with compassionate, expert support every step of the way.

📋

Home Safety Assessment

We evaluate your home environment and recommend modifications to reduce fall risks and improve your ability to move safely throughout your living space.
